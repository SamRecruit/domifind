FAQ

Good questions

Is it really free to start?

Yes. Sending your brief and receiving your shortlist costs nothing. Our fee applies only once you've chosen a property and decided to go ahead.

How fast will I get my shortlist?

Usually within a few days, depending on how specific your brief is and the market in your chosen area. We'll keep you updated along the way.

Can I search from abroad?

Absolutely. Many clients relocate to Cyprus. We can do video viewings, check areas for you and organise everything before you arrive.

Do you only show properties from one agency?

No. We search across the entire Cyprus market, so you get the best options, not the ones a single agency needs to sell.

Do you also help with rentals?

Yes. Long-term rentals in all districts, for families, professionals and companies relocating staff.
